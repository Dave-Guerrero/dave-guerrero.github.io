Executive Summary — Vantor received 1,201 website-sourced leads between January and April 2026. Of these, 409 (34.1%) qualify as genuine business opportunities (MQL or Working), while 444 (37%) were disqualified — 91% of those due solely to bad contact information. The US leads in volume (219 leads, 47% quality rate), while India (71%), Germany (59%), and the UK (54%) punch above their weight on quality. Paid Search launched in February and is still in early optimization with only $2,500 USD invested to date; at current CPL, costs are well below industry benchmarks with significant room to scale while maintaining efficiency. Attribution gaps in untracked traffic are partly structural: Apple’s ITP blocks UTM cookie storage on iOS/Safari, and government agency browsers frequently block cookie persistence — both are industry-wide limitations in Vantor’s sector.

Q4 + Q5

Which channels drive the most leads — and which produce the best quality?

Context Paid Search context: Campaigns launched in February 2026. Total investment to date: ~$2,500 USD across approximately three weeks of active spend. Current CPL is well below B2B SaaS industry benchmarks ($200–$500+ per quality lead). The program is still in active optimization — geo-exclusions have already been applied — and early-stage quality rates are expected to improve as audience signals accumulate and negative keyword lists mature.
Context Attribution gap caveat: A significant share of “untracked” leads is structural, not operational. Apple’s Intelligent Tracking Prevention (ITP) blocks UTM storage in Safari cookies across all iOS/macOS devices. Government agencies frequently disable cookie persistence entirely. Both are endemic in Vantor’s defense, aerospace, and public-sector audience. Server-side tagging or first-party data capture (e.g., form hidden fields storing UTM at submission time) can mitigate this, but full attribution is unachievable in this vertical.

Top Five Prioritized Recommendations

1

Implement server-side UTM capture on the Contact Us form — before scaling spend

Apple ITP and government cookie-blocking make client-side UTM tracking structurally unreliable for Vantor’s audience. The fix is to capture UTM parameters as hidden form fields at submission time, storing them server-side before any cookie expires. This is a one-sprint engineering task and will dramatically improve attribution fidelity for the 19.7% of leads currently dark — leads that carry the highest quality rate (48.3%) in the dataset.

● Impact: High — foundational for all future spend optimization
2

Continue Paid Search geo-exclusion rollout and add negative keyword layers

Geo-exclusions already applied are the right move. At $2,500 total spend, CPL is well below industry benchmarks — the program has room to scale. Priority next actions: expand negative keywords (student, academic, university, free, research grant), add audience layering (in-market B2B professional segments), and test dedicated landing pages per vertical (Defense, GIS, SaaS). February’s 45.8% quality rate shows the channel is capable of strong performance when properly targeted.

● Impact: High — scalable, below-benchmark CPL with clear optimization path
3

Prioritize the 67 Defense & Intelligence quality leads as Tier-1 pipeline immediately

Government — Defense & Intelligence is Vantor’s highest-alignment vertical: mission-critical imagery needs, institutional budgets, and long contract cycles. These 67 leads should receive immediate SDR outreach with defense-specific messaging, not generic nurture. Cross-referencing with the 37 quality leads carrying budgets above $250,000 identifies the single highest-revenue-potential cohort in the entire dataset.

● Impact: High — highest near-term revenue potential
4

Add company-email validation and CAPTCHA to the Contact Us form

402 of 444 disqualifications (91%) were coded “bad information.” Blocking free-domain email addresses (Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail) and adding a CAPTCHA step would eliminate the majority of bot and low-intent form submissions before they enter the CRM. This reduces SDR triage time, improves data hygiene, and gives monthly quality-rate metrics a more accurate baseline.

● Impact: Medium-High — reduces operational noise immediately
5

Test LinkedIn Sponsored Content at modest budget — signal is promising

LinkedIn delivered a 40% quality rate on only five leads — a small but directionally strong signal. Given that Vantor’s ICP (VP/C-suite/Director at LATAM tech companies, defense contractors, and government-adjacent firms) is precisely LinkedIn’s highest-value targeting surface, a $3,000–$5,000 test with job-function and seniority targeting could yield significantly more data. If quality rates hold above 35%, it becomes a primary channel to scale alongside Paid Search.

● Impact: Medium — high potential, low-cost validation possible
