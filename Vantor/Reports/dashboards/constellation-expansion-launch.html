Q2

How did paid distribution perform across audiences and creative?

LINKEDIN · 4-VARIANT TEST · 18.7M IMPRESSIONS
A 4-variant test across two audiences (USG & International Government) and two creative formats (Image & Video) revealed a clear strategic finding: scale image for awareness, deploy video for narrative depth.
The Strategic Finding

Image delivers scale; video delivers story.

Image format with Maximum Delivery bidding unlocked 20×+ scale compared to video at near-identical spend — the ideal layer for top-of-funnel awareness.

Video earned ~99K total views per campaign — fewer impressions, but each one carried the mission narrative, the tradeoff eliminated, and the constellation vision that static imagery cannot convey.

Best practice for next launch: image for reach and frequency, video for narrative depth with warm or high-value segments.

Audience Targeting

USG targeting hit the bullseye.

The US Government audience delivered the campaign's most concentrated buyer-base reach: US Army, USAF, Navy, Marines, Booz Allen, Leidos, DHS, State Department, and the Department of War were all in the top 15 companies reached.

DC-Baltimore metro alone drove 14.4% of US impressions — the federal decision-making corridor delivered as designed.

What This Means

The campaign's true pipeline contribution is likely 3× the directly confirmed count.

Direct attribution (5 confirmed) captures only the leads that came in via the campaign's tracked channels (email blast, LinkedIn organic post, X organic). It misses the leads who saw the CNBC interview, read the Aviation Week piece, or attended the Vantor product webinar — and then visited vantor.com to submit an inquiry days later.

The attribution analysis identifies 10 additional leads with high-confidence campaign signal. Combined with the 5 direct conversions, that's 15 leads in 30 days — a more realistic measure of the launch's pipeline influence.
